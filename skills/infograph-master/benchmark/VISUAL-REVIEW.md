# Visual quality review protocol (V1.2)

This protocol elaborates the existing rubric, not a new weighted benchmark. Do not double count representation, narrative or interaction under visual. Freeze task applicability before viewing outputs. Review screenshots blind to model and arm; evaluate interaction separately on runnable artifacts.

| Dimension | Evidence to inspect | Adequate (3) | Strong (4) | Exceptional (5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Color | semantic mapping, contrast, focal hierarchy | legible, consistent mapping | intentional restrained or expressive palette appropriate to brief | distinctive art direction with precise semantic and attentional control |
| Typography | hierarchy, line length, numerical alignment, script coverage | readable hierarchy | consistent rhythm and appropriate type choices | refined hierarchy and rhythm under realistic content stress |
| Composition | alignment, grouping, density, whitespace, reading path | coherent grouping | deliberate visual hierarchy and balanced density | composition makes complex information unusually easy to navigate |
| Material | surface, depth, texture, transparency | no readability harm | material reinforces purpose and hierarchy | material is integral to expression without obscuring evidence |
| Representation craft | label placement, marks, legends, visual precision | readable marks and labels | economical, precise execution | unusually clear treatment of complex relationships |
| Art direction | cohesion, specificity, contextual appropriateness | coherent style | deliberate and recognizable visual language | distinctive, coherent expression that serves the task better than plausible alternatives |

Scores 0–2: 0 absent or unusable; 1 major defects; 2 partially successful with material weaknesses. Use null for unavailable evidence, not zero. A 5 requires concrete evidence, not personal preference or novelty alone. Minimalism, maximalism, editorial, technical, tactile and other directions are equally eligible.

For each applicable dimension record observation, location, consequence, severity, proposed repair and confidence. Distinguish observed defects from taste preferences. Do not infer font coverage, interaction behavior or causal correctness from screenshots alone.

## Review procedure

1. Read brief, audience, task and constraints; record intended reading path.
2. Inspect desktop and mobile at native scale; inspect stress samples where available.
3. Record strengths and defects independently before comparing alternatives.
4. Compare at least two genuinely different directions for substantial design work; explain tradeoffs, not merely color changes.
5. Use geometry, contrast and pixel-diff reports as supporting evidence, not aesthetic scores.
6. Assign the existing visual rubric score holistically with explicit rationale. The six dimensions above are diagnostic prompts, not independent weights. Representation correctness, narrative effectiveness and interaction functionality retain their original rubric dimensions.
7. Escalate disagreements of two or more scale points for adjudication; retain original ratings and rationale.
8. Never promote an unreviewed artifact or a hard-fail artifact based on visual polish.

## Scope

No actual human review, expert validation or model comparison is claimed by this protocol. Screenshot review cannot establish usability or accessibility conformance. The existing benchmark rubric and hard-fail policy remain authoritative.
