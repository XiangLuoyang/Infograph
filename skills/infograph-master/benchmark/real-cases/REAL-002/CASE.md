# REAL-002 — Resource circulation / allocation bottleneck

Date: 2026-11-26. Status: **owner accepted V2 for this workflow; candidate baseline, not Golden**. Source: real-user iterative executive HTML report; public record is generalized and omits organization, geography, exact metrics, product names and original HTML/artwork.

## Brief
Explain how a resource pool can be sufficient in aggregate while particular needs remain unmet. Reveal the possible structural allocation problem, its effect on cycle efficiency and downstream economic exposure, and propose governance actions. The reader must distinguish actual evidence from the qualitative mechanism.
