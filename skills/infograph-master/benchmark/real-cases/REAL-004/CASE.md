## Transfer contract
1. Distinguish coverage from depth. An isometric stacked bar is not automatically a mechanism metaphor.
2. Narrative interaction: distinguish parallel IA from cognitive progression; use a persistent scene only when it helps; offer free exploration with shared semantic/data state.
3. Choreography: actions must change meaningful objects, relationships, evidence or inspection context; not only decorative highlights.
4. Art direction: for justified high-fidelity visual concepts, establish composition/material/light and inspect the concept before committing to geometry and renderer. Keep data and labels independently editable; never force image generation.
5. Motion completeness: inventory physically salient moving objects and material behavior; assess static/rigid/deformable/fluid/optical roles, motion semantics and fidelity. No false quantitative animation; reduced-motion alternative.
6. Global spatial composition: macro viewport/navigation/scroll contract, meso per-slice hierarchy, micro chart/object/material. Preserve accepted illustration when changing layout. Stable desktop mode is appropriate for this presentation workspace, not universal.
