# Candidate promotion gate

Statuses: candidate, engineering-verified, audience-validated. Never infer a higher status from a lower one.

Engineering verification requires a recorded commit, successful Python checks, all 18 browser smoke cases passing, review of all captured screenshots and DOM diagnostics, correct source data and units, visible limitations, keyboard and reduced-motion checks, and manual chart-label and material review. A screenshot capture is not pixel regression; baseline approval and image comparison are future work. The current smoke test does not cover every control or all interaction states. Record exceptions and reviewers. No promotion without evidence.

Audience validation additionally requires task-specific comprehension, retrieval and decision tests against a comparator. Do not equate aesthetic preference with comprehension.

Current status: six candidates; browser workflow submitted, results not observed. No promotion.
