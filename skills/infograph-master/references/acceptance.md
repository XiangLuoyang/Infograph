# Acceptance
Blocking: incorrect numbers, unsupported causal claims, hidden material caveats, unauthorized data disclosure, broken essential controls, misleading domain geometry or units. Repair before claiming completion.
Check evidence lineage and totals; chart axes, labels, scales and color semantics; contrast and keyboard focus; no clipping/overlap at target widths; typography and hierarchy; material legibility; meaningful motion and reduced-motion; filters, drilldown, reset, exports, empty/missing/extreme data; console errors and offline dependencies. Inspect real screenshots if browser available, otherwise explicitly mark visual QA untested. For narrative reports use repo:skills/narrative-design/tests/protocol.md; for monitoring use lookup and alert tasks. Record pass/fail/not-run, evidence and fix. Do not fabricate test results.

## Core design principle acceptance
Always read design-principles.md and apply DP01-DP05 proportionately. For substantial work record design and review evidence described there. Verify layer fitness and semantic independence, whole-scene material coherence, target-device composition and control reach, actual reader-task interaction, and preservation of accepted baseline elements. A new artifact records no prior baseline; a static artifact records why interaction is unnecessary. Review actual foreground/background pairs, including selected, hover, focus and disabled states where applicable; inspect gradients, images and transparent layers visually. No overflow is not proof of usable composition. User-reported unreadable essential content or broken essential controls is blocking until verified repair; disclose untested hosts. Visual review and CI are distinct; never imply human approval or generalized effectiveness from functional tests alone.
