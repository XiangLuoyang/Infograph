# Acceptance
Blocking: incorrect numbers, unsupported causal claims, hidden material caveats, unauthorized data disclosure, broken essential controls, misleading domain geometry or units. Repair before claiming completion.
Check evidence lineage and totals; chart axes, labels, scales and color semantics; contrast and keyboard focus; no clipping/overlap at target widths; typography and hierarchy; material legibility; meaningful motion and reduced-motion; filters, drilldown, reset, exports, empty/missing/extreme data; console errors and offline dependencies. Inspect real screenshots if browser available, otherwise explicitly mark visual QA untested. For narrative reports use repo:skills/narrative-design/tests/protocol.md; for monitoring use lookup and alert tasks. Record pass/fail/not-run, evidence and fix. Do not fabricate test results.
