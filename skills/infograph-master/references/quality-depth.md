# Quality depth — integrated production contract V0.1 (REAL-004)
Mandatory applicability review for substantial dashboards and authored interactive explanations, including revisions. Complements system-level refactoring, narrative-design, VMI, reference reconstruction and DP01–DP05. It does not replace semantic validation or require decorative motion. Aim for first-pass depth, not more features.

## QD01 Narrative director
For a presentation/decision task, use repo:skills/narrative-design/SKILL.md first. Compare candidate thesis/spines. Each act declares cognitive-before, question, claim, evidence IDs, cognitive-after, dominant visual focus, transition reason and next decision. A tab list is not a storyboard. An unsupported cause must be framed as hypothesis. Material evidence/caveats cannot be concealed in playback. For mixed presentation/exploration, define director and explorer as modes of the same data model, not duplicate calculations: preserve scenario/filter context or visibly declare a scope change; provide reversible navigation, pause, manual advance and direct inspection. Pure monitoring/lookup: explicitly decline narrative where it harms the task.
Review: naked-title/retell test, claim-evidence links, act transitions, mode roundtrip and static reading path. Independent retell is separate from self-review.

## QD02 Interaction choreography
Author a transition matrix: trigger, stable target IDs, before state, after state, semantic payload, evidence link, focus, duration/easing rationale, interrupt/reverse rule, keyboard/touch and motion-off alternative. Distinguish functional filtering, inspection, scenario change, staged revelation and mechanism motion. Test the removal question: if motion is removed, what meaning or task is lost? If none, prefer static/low-motion. Do not use an animation to imply an unsupported causal relation. State is shared and authoritative; rapid clicks, back, reset, resize and playback interruption must not desynchronize labels/objects. Review states and transitions, not just the existence of controls.

## QD03 Visual art direction
Compare at least two materially different directions and the ordinary non-metaphorical representation; record why the chosen one serves the task. For a rich scene, define dominant silhouette, viewpoint, relative scale, spatial arrangement, palette, material families, surface detail, lighting and label safe zones. Inspect an actual concept artifact (sketch, image or rendered study) before final implementation when feasible. Concept artwork is not mandatory for ordinary charts. Route mechanism selection to VMI and physical/semantic mapping to dynamic-visual-metaphor.md. Route concept-to-code to reference-reconstruction.md; author layer/object manifest with stable IDs, bounding regions, depth order, anchors, material/lighting and motion requirements. Choose per-layer renderer, retain semantic labels, exact evidence and provenance. Never upload private artwork without permission. No image tool available? Use a purposeful authored visual study or disclose fidelity limits; do not silently substitute a flat generic shape for essential photoreal fidelity.
Review concept vs output at matched view/scale and states: silhouette, perspective, material, lighting, coherence of neighboring UI, text/contrast and target-device legibility. Functional checks cannot certify aesthetics.

## QD04 Motion completeness
Inventory salient scene objects before build: static structure, rigid moving part, articulated mechanism, deformable surface, liquid/gas, optical/emissive effect or semantic overlay. For each record stable ID, physical behavior, state/trigger, required motion or justified static, renderer, occlusion/anchor rule, qualitative vs calibrated mapping, reduced-motion state. Review missing motion as well as excess motion. Preserve physical identity: e.g. rotate a wheel in its plane, retain thickness and lighting; animate liquid surface/texture/flow within container boundaries and preserve glass reflections. These are examples, not a default factory template. Motion speed/level/force cannot imply measured values without mapping and evidence. Independent background motion should not distract or conflict with reading or pause. Compare motion frames and actual reduced-motion state; screenshots alone cannot prove continuity. If an essential object remains static or has inconsistent perspective, repair or report as unresolved.

## QD05 Global spatial composition
Macro: enumerate every primary tab/route and secondary slice; declare presentation, exploration, monitoring or lookup mode; define viewport target, shell, navigation hierarchy, stable content region, global/local filter placement, scroll owner, focus/return policy. Compare fixed workspace, natural scroll and hybrid; choose by task. No universal one-screen mandate. For presentation workspaces prefer theme slices over unbounded scrolling; allow table-local scrolling and mobile reflow when justified. Never hide evidence by clipping. Meso: scene hierarchy, evidence/controls, density per slice. Micro: typography, chart marks, motion layers, interaction targets. Protect accepted scenes when shell changes. Test every route and slice at target widths/heights, transition continuity, scroll reach, keyboard/touch, overflow, readable labels, and task completion. Intentional differences in layout mode must be explicit, not accidental by content length.

## QD06 Depth review
Use two separate dispositions: coverage (all applicable areas addressed, facts/functions preserved) and depth (actual evidence of narrative quality, semantic transitions, expressive visual craft, physical-motion integrity, spatial consistency). No averaged score may hide a material defect. For each applicable QD area author design evidence and revision-bound rendered review evidence; report pass/fail/not-run; explain nonapplicability from task. Flag low depth even if every checkbox exists: modules instead of story; state highlight instead of meaningful action; generic geometries in place of concept; static liquid or flattened rigid parts; inconsistent scroll ownership; isolated card polish versus coherent workspace. Repair highest severity; compare against accepted layer baseline. Review full composition, then per-object details. Report independent user approval separately and never infer from CI. Missing evidence blocks declared depth completion, not honest partial delivery.

## Execution handoff
Load this document before form selection, then specialized references on demand. Record a compact plan and review with stable revision and evidence refs. Optional engine/quality-depth.mjs audits declared applicability, pre-build and review records; see engine/test-quality-depth.mjs. A checker does not author a storyboard, inspect actual pixels, judge beauty or verify evidence. This is a manual production contract plus a structural audit, not a new renderer or automatic release gate. REAL-004 is one owner-approved case; held-out trials are still required.
