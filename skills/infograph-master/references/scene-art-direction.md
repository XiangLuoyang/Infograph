# Scene-level art direction — advisory route selection

Use before authoring a substantial mechanism scene, alongside the plain alternative and capability opportunities. Select a grammar based on what the reader needs to understand, not the renderer or a desire for novelty. This is guidance, not an automatic classifier, new production stage or release gate.

| Grammar | Prefer when | Avoid when | Existing route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Node composition | Multiple entities or relations need explicit traceability, lookup, comparison or network reading | The message depends on a memorable single focal mechanism and isolated icons obscure it | canonical VMI scene and composition |
| Hero scene | One recognizable physical mechanism anchors the explanation; other roles may be live annotations | Distinct physical endpoints, multiple independent entities or dense quantitative comparisons are essential | authored geometry within supported canonical scope; otherwise concept-art/reference/custom hybrid with explicit separate verification |
| Physical assembly | Multiple source objects really form a spatially connected mechanism; ports, geometry and topology are supported by evidence | Abstract allocation, competition, causal or symbolic relations are being mistaken for physical connections | existing geometry/topology or reference WebGL/hybrid within supported bounds |
| Cinematic scene | Environment, perspective and staged explanation materially help an audience follow a mechanism over time | Lookup, exact comparison or static evidence is the priority | existing concept-art, narrative director, interaction choreography and motion-completeness routes; experimental |

Compare at least a plain representation and a materially different plausible grammar before investing in a rich scene. Identify essential semantic objects/edges, what must stay interactive, focal hierarchy, shared view/camera, consistent lighting/material, label zones and responsive reading order. Use actual source and design DNA. Keep live quantities, evidence, controls and essential labels outside baked artwork; map stable IDs to layers or annotations; do not infer physical connectivity, values or motion.

## Integration boundary

Existing selected canonical VMI scenes still require their exact compiled fragments and normal browser evidence. A manually authored hero or hybrid is not an engine-generated VMI fragment. Do not replace an assembled canonical slot with an approximation, claim custom graphics are compiler output, or duplicate a scene solely to satisfy a gate. If the desired grammar exceeds canonical scene scope, use an applicable existing specialist/custom route with explicit disposition and evidence; revise the production representation choice or surface the unsupported integration as a blocker. Do not silently bypass a required VMI stage or slot. A future canonical hybrid adapter requires separate implementation and validation; this guidance does not supply one.

Before final selection, compare actual renders at target viewport/state and retain a readable static alternative. Review semantic/interaction parity separately from aesthetic preference. Real-case evidence should determine further renderer work.
