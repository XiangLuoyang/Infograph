# Color art direction
Harmony is a compositional hypothesis, not a universal beauty law. Six hue relationships: monochrome, analogous, complementary, split complementary, triadic, neutral plus accent. Use neutral/support/accent area guide 75/20/5 only as starting heuristic, not measured pixel coverage. Visual weight depends on area, local contrast, saturation, position and surrounding content. Reserve high contrast for focal claims. Warm/cool meanings vary by culture and context. Preserve supplied brand hex exactly; restrict role if contrast fails, do not silently recolor. Keep semantic data palettes independent. Overlay helper approximates encoded sRGB source-over on one opaque backdrop; actual browser compositing, blur, gradients and layered materials need inspection. Eight candidate directions are generated, not reverse-engineered or audience validated.
