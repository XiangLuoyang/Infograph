# Cross-domain composition studies
Six source-backed cases across five domains. Observations are paraphrases of institutional or creator descriptions; composition tags and transfer rules are analyst hypotheses, not measured effects. Magazine/editorial and independent photography audits remain pending.

## CC01 The Red Studio
https://www.moma.org/collection/works/78389/

Common red field, sparse contours for furniture, detailed artworks and clock axis.

Composition: Field + axial + focus. Transfer: Reduce contextual chrome while retaining evidence detail. Limit: Do not suppress necessary labels.

## CC02 Villa Savoye
https://www.fondationlecorbusier.fr/en/work-architecture/achievements-villa-savoye-and-gardeners-lodge-poissy-france-1928-1931/

Ramp and staircase offer routes through successive viewpoints.

Composition: Linear + frame + threshold. Transfer: Maintain object continuity across optional guided scenes. Limit: Provide direct navigation.

## CC03 Musica Viva 1958–1960
https://swissgrid.posterhouse.org/

Flexible grid accommodates geometric image, dense text and unequal columns.

Composition: Modular + asymmetric + hero. Transfer: Keep alignment anchors while varying chapter composition. Limit: Do not equate grid consistency with identical cards.

## CC04 Weniger Lärm 1960
https://access.posterhouse.org/exhibition/swiss-grid-2/

Museum describes grid rotated 50 degrees for tension.

Composition: Diagonal + hero. Transfer: Use local directional exception for a meaningful disruption. Limit: Keep data axes and essential text legible.

## CC05 Citizen Kane
https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/f4c92833-af39-5cd6-a41e-8df5933d0dc1/citizen-kane

Deep-focus photography keeps foreground and background in focus.

Composition: Layered + frame. Transfer: Keep primary evidence and contextual evidence simultaneously readable. Limit: Avoid occlusion and forced focus.

## CC06 Population Mountains
https://pudding.cool/2018/12/3d-cities-story/

Introduces population dots, extrudes them into blocks, zooms out and compares regions at comparable scale.

Composition: Field + layered + small-multiples. Transfer: Introduce encoding before changing representation; preserve comparative scales. Limit: Height must encode actual quantity; provide flat accessible alternative.
