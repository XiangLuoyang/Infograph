# Industrial material intelligence
Eight authored surface references, three edge descriptors and four assembly descriptors. Use a shared spacing system, consistent seams, functional markings, stable reading surfaces and restrained decoration. IndustrialPlan emits opaque CSS surface colors, brushed/blasted repeating patterns, rounded corners and inset/stacked shadow approximations. Shine is catalog metadata; chamfer and seamed assembly are not geometrically rendered. Glass is opaque fallback. No PBR, physical tolerances, real optics, browser or audience validation. Bead-blasted metal extends the prior atlas but is not merged into it. Fifteen inline checks passed. Reading queue (not freshly reviewed): https://www.vitsoe.com/us/about/good-design and https://pbr-book.org/4ed/.
