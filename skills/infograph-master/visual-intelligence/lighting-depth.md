# Lighting and depth
Global light profile and elevation tokens. Azimuth is clockwise from screen north toward the light source; cast shadow points away. CSS approximation combines contact and directional shadow; elevation is a design token, not physical height. Ambient reduces shadow opacity, softness increases blur. Temperature is metadata only, not applied to color. Four illustrative presets. Keep semantic hierarchy separate from elevation; no essential information encoded solely by shadows. Audit text contrast independently. No occlusion, ray tracing, ambient occlusion simulation, browser validation or perceptual calibration.
