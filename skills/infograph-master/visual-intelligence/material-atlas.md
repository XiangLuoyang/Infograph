# Material Atlas
12 reference families and 36 candidate family/finish combinations. Physical references and abstract digital references are distinguished. Finishes include treatments, surface states and construction descriptors; this is a design taxonomy, not a scientific material classification. Geometry and rendering are independent fields. Six normalized design descriptors reuse the foundations vocabulary; they are not calibrated PBR values. No automatic parameter inference, physical compatibility verification, texture assets or renderer. Eleven inline checks passed. Suggested associations require contextual review. Existing V1.1 material catalog remains separate.
