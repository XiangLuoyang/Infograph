# Progressive disclosure
Eight candidate patterns. Essential items must appear at entry and remain in each scene's declared visible set. Fact and inference require evidence references; prerequisites must be available by the scene, and cycles and unreachable items are rejected. Same-scene prerequisites are allowed; no within-scene order enforcement. Static order is first introduction order. No DOM renderer, scroll controller, evidence truth check, or audience test. Eleven inline checks passed. Reading queue (not freshly reviewed): Segel & Heer, Narrative Visualization (2010), https://vis.stanford.edu/papers/narrative ; Nielsen Norman Group, Progressive Disclosure, https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/ .
