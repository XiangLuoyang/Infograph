# Semantic color
Categorical: stable IDs, unordered hues, direct labels or shapes. Greedy maximin search across normal and three full-severity simulated vision modes; deterministic but not globally optimal. Background contrast filter 3:1 is a candidate graphic check, not text certification. Diverging: odd count, neutral midpoint, symmetric OKLCH lightness schedule; map to meaningful numeric center and explicit bounds, disclose asymmetric domains. Unknown separate from zero. Status: use text/icons and domain meaning, never infer good/bad from sign alone. CVD simulation uses Machado et al. 2009 full-severity matrices in linear sRGB, clips output; not a clinical model or guarantee. OKLab Euclidean distances are heuristics, no universal pass threshold. Reference: https://www.inf.ufrgs.br/~oliveira/pubs_files/CVD_Simulation/CVD_Simulation.html (not freshly retrieved).
