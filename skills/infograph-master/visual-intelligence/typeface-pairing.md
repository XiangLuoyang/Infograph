# Typeface pairing
Sources reviewed: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/cc-by-sa/knowledge/modules/choosing_type/lessons/pairing_typefaces_based_on_their_construction_using_the_font_matrix/content.md ; https://source.typekit.com/source-han-serif/ ; https://ccjktype.fonts.adobe.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/iuc39-lunde-s10t3.pdf . Shape model and stroke contrast are pairing heuristics, not measured aesthetic scores. Compare coordinated families, deliberate contrast and single-family hierarchy. Evaluate actual x-height, width, weight, baseline and language coverage. CJK and Latin harmonization may require scaling and baseline adjustment. Eight candidate recipes, no fonts bundled, no license or availability certification. Dense mode substitutes body face for heading; availability report includes original candidate faces. Do not apply monospace to all numeric content by default. Browser and specimen review pending.
