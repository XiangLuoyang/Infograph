# VMI roadmap
- P1 implemented seed: source register, 16 authored directed predicates, 9 source-linked example mechanisms, bounded structural search and declared concept gate; Master Skill route. Tests synthetic.
- P2 planned: larger source-backed knowledge corpus, functional synonyms with direction/arity constraints, multi-abstraction and open discovery protocol; negative and unseen fixtures.
- P3 planned: creative transformations (combine, substitute, juxtapose, reconstruct) with provenance, semantic counterexamples and candidate critique.
- P4 planned: design-DNA adaptation and authored scene IR; bridge to existing reference reconstruction, motion and specialist renderers.
- P5 planned: interactive studio, matched screenshot/interaction checks, independent reverse-comprehension evaluation and blind baseline comparison.
No formal release, full corpus coverage, model benchmark, autonomous discovery or art quality claim. Source review and mapping correctness remain human/agent responsibilities.
