# Narrative atlas (provisional taxonomy)
## Spines
Question: recurring inquiry; Object: stable entity; Process: lifecycle or flow; Spatial: geographic or semantic coordinates; Argument: claim and supporting reasons.
## Archetypes
Chronology, Transformation, Milestone; Pyramid, SCQA, Investigation; Decomposition, Comparison, Causal Chain, System Loop; Gap Analysis, Scenario, Trade-off, Opportunity; Spatial Journey, Entity Journey.
Choose by audience task, not visual preference. Spine binds content; archetype orders it.
## Grammar
Orient, Assert, Evidence, Compare, Contrast, Question, Explain, Qualify, Synthesize, Resolve, Bridge, Recap. Each scene may use several atoms but should have one primary cognitive goal.
## Transitions
Zoom-in/out, Temporal, Causal (only with evidence), Contrast, Perspective, Flow, Callback, Decision. Preserve relevant context and object identity.
## Rhythm and resolution
Establish → Intensify → Release → Resolve is an optional rhythm, not a universal sequence. Alternate dense evidence with concise synthesis. Offer executive summary, argument and evidence layers; allow direct access to evidence. Do not hide material caveats in appendices.
